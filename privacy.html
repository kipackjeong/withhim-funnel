1. The short version

With Him has no account requirement, no analytics SDK, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The Bible, your notes, your highlights, your reading position, your alarms and your shield settings are all stored on your own device. Two things can reach a server, and both only because you chose to send them: feedback you type, and — if you sign in — the identity Apple hands over.

The rest of this page is the same statement said precisely enough to be relied on.

2. Your voice

Audio is never recorded, never stored, and never transmitted. Reading a verse aloud is matched by Apple's on-device speech recogniser, on your phone. No audio file is created, nothing is uploaded, and there is no server that could receive it even in principle.

What the app keeps from a spoken reading is the outcome, not the sound: that a verse was completed, when, and by which method (spoken or typed). That record is stored on the device and is what the reading history and the alarm's own logic are built from.

The microphone is used only while a verse is on screen waiting to be read, and iOS shows its own indicator the entire time it is live. If the on-device recogniser is unavailable, the app falls back to typing rather than sending audio anywhere.

3. What stays on the device

All of the following is held in the app's own storage on your phone, and in whatever backup you have configured with Apple — we have no access to any of it:

  • Highlights, notes, bookmarks and reading positions.
  • Reading history and which passages have been completed.
  • Alarms, their schedules, and the record of which ones were answered.
  • Focus Shield configuration and the apps you selected for it.
  • Reading preferences: theme, text size, translation, language.
  • The Bible text itself, which ships inside the app and needs no network at all.

Some of this is shared between the app and its extensions through a private App Group container on the device, which is what lets the shield and the alarm agree with the app about what has been read. That container is local; it is not a sync service.

4. Screen Time data

The Focus Shield uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) framework. When you choose which apps to shield, iOS hands the app opaque tokens rather than the identities of the apps — by Apple's design we cannot see which apps you picked, and the selection cannot leave the device. Usage figures shown in the app are rendered by a sandboxed Apple extension that is not permitted to return data to us either.

5. What we do collect

Two things, both only when you act to send them.

Feedback you type

Settings → Send feedback turns what you write into an issue in our development tracker. No identifier is attached, so it is not linked to you or to your device. Do not put anything in that box you would not want stored — including your email address, unless you want a reply and are content for it to be recorded.

Identity, if you sign in

Signing in is entirely optional and no feature requires it. It is offered only through Sign in with Apple. If you use it, we receive and store the Apple user identifier, an email address — Apple's private relay address unless you choose to share your real one — and the name Apple provides on the first authorisation. That is the whole account record. No highlight, note, bookmark, alarm or reading position is ever sent with it.

6. Purchases

Payment is processed entirely by Apple. We never see, receive or store your card details, billing address, or any other payment instrument.

To know whether a subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription-infrastructure provider. RevenueCat receives the App Store purchase record and an anonymous identifier it generates for the installation, and returns whether the subscription is entitled. That identifier is not linked to your name or email unless you have signed in and we associate it.

7. Tracking and advertising

There is none. No advertising identifier is read, no cross-app or cross-site tracking is performed, no data is sold or shared with data brokers, and no third-party analytics SDK is embedded in the app. The App Tracking Transparency prompt does not appear because there is nothing to ask for. The app's privacy manifest, which Apple publishes on the App Store listing, says the same thing in Apple's own format.

8. Who else is involved

Three parties, each with a narrow role:

  • Apple — the App Store, payment, Sign in with Apple, on-device speech recognition, alarms and Screen Time, under Apple's own privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat — subscription status only, as described above.
  • Supabase — hosts the account record for anyone who signs in, and nothing else.

There is no fourth. Nothing is sold, rented or shared for anyone's marketing purposes.

9. How long anything is kept

On-device data lasts as long as the app is installed; deleting the app removes it, and we have no copy to remove on our side. An account record is kept until you delete the account. Feedback is kept for as long as the issue it created is useful, which for a bug report may be a long time.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or erase personal data we hold, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to a supervisory authority. Because the only personal data we hold is an optional account record, most of these resolve to a single action: Settings → Account → Delete account, inside the app, which erases that record. If you would rather have it done by hand, or want a copy first, write to us and it will be handled.

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those terms are defined under California law.

11. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and it will be deleted.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. A change that widens what is collected will be announced in the app before it takes effect — not left in a document nobody re-reads.

13. Contact

Write to support@withhim.app, or use Settings → Send feedback inside the app.
