On Android, Plus is required for the main app, not only the features listed above. Plans are offered monthly and annually; the store purchase screen shows the price, billing period and any available trial before you confirm. 8. What the alarm can promise

With Him is not a safety-critical alarm and must not be used as one. Do not rely on it alone to wake you for anything where being late has consequences — a flight, a shift, medication, a medical appointment.

On iPhone, the alarm is scheduled through Apple's system alarm services, and whether it fires depends on iOS, on the device being powered on and functional, on system permissions remaining granted, and on the device not being in a state that suppresses alerts. Android alarms likewise depend on the device and system permissions. None of that is under our control. Keep an independent alarm for anything that matters.

Speech recognition is likewise a best-effort match, not a proof of reading. It can fail on background noise, on an unfamiliar accent, or when the on-device recogniser is unavailable — which is why a typing fallback and an escape route always exist. The app is a habit, not a lock.

9. The Focus Shield

On iPhone, the Focus Shield operates through Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) framework and requires your explicit authorisation, which you can withdraw at any time in iOS Settings. Withdrawing it lifts the shield. The shield restricts apps you have selected yourself; it is a chosen friction, not a parental control, and it should not be relied on as a restriction someone else cannot remove. Android Manage Screen Time uses a separately consented Accessibility service and Usage access, which you can revoke in Android Settings. It observes package-level foreground changes and timing locally, not screen content; see the Android privacy details.
