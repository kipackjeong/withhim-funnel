Welcome to With Him.

A verse at the beginning of the day. A moment with Scripture when the feed has held you too long. Start wherever you are.

Open With Him in the App Store

The install happens in the App Store; visiting this page does not install the app. For iPhone with iOS 26 or later.

Your first three steps

  1. 1

    Install from the App Store.

    Open the listing on your iPhone and download With Him. It’s free to download.

  2. 2

    Open the Bible.

    Choose a passage and read. The complete Bible is free, including offline reading.

  3. 3

    Make a morning for it.

    Set a wake alarm in the app and meet your first verse when the day begins.
