What to actually expect

No date, because any date given now would be a guess wearing a suit. Here is what's true instead.

The order things get built

  • The Bible reader first. All 66 books offline, search, cross-references, highlights, notes. It has to be good enough to be someone's only Bible app before anything else matters.
  • Then the alarm. The verification engine and the Voice-Dismissed Alarm. It isn't finished until it has run thirty real mornings in a row without a missed fire.
  • Then the Focus Shield. This one depends on Apple approving a specific entitlement, on a timeline nobody outside Apple controls. It's possible the alarm ships first and the shield follows later — you'll be told if that happens rather than finding out at launch.
  • Then onboarding, subscriptions, and polish. The unglamorous part that decides whether any of the rest survives contact with a real first morning.

What you'll get, and how often

  • Occasional progress notes. Roughly monthly, sometimes less. What's built, what broke, what's taking longer than expected. Not a newsletter and not a drip campaign.
  • A TestFlight invitation, in a wave. You'll be asked before you're added. The first wave is small because Apple caps external testing and reviews every build.
  • The launch email, with the founding lifetime rate of $49 valid for that first week, for the first 500 people on this list.

And an ordinary reminder: if you decide at any point this isn't for you, one click in any email removes you completely. That's not a hurdle and there's no exit sequence behind it.

One thing that would help

There is no advertising budget here and no plan to build one before launch. The reason is simple: the person this is for can't be targeted by an ad platform. "Practising Christian who owns a study Bible they don't open and has a broken reading streak" is an interior state, not an interest category, and no amount of money buys accurate reach into it.

The people who know who fits are the ones who've heard the confession over coffee. If someone came to mind while you were reading any of this, send them the demo — not the sales page, the demo. Thirty seconds of reading a verse out loud explains this better than any amount of copy, including all of the copy you just read.

No referral rewards, no leaderboard, no points. Just a link, if it's useful to someone.
