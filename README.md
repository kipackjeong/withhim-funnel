# With Him — App Store site

Static site for **With Him**, a published iPhone Bible app. The alarm hands you a short verse to read aloud or type; a chosen Focus Shield pauses selected apps after scrolling. The Bible reader remains free and works offline.

Live at <https://kipackjeong.github.io/withhim-funnel/>. The App Store listing is <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/with-him-wake-in-the-words/id6795353533>.

## Pages

`index.html` is the main landing page, implemented from the approved **A / Unfold** prototype (`admin/landing-prototype/v-unfold.html` in the app repository). `join.html` keeps its existing URL but is now the download page; `thanks.html` is a welcome and sharing guide, not proof of installation. The download, support, privacy, and terms pages retain their existing content, URLs, and shared light styles; the landing redesign does not change `assets/shared.css` or `assets/legal.css`.

The earlier pre-launch waitlist has ended. All download actions go directly to the App Store. The landing page preserves the established `#mechanism`, `#reader`, `#pricing`, `#faq`, and `#close` anchors, including links from the download pages.

The 1320×2868 raws are resampled to 1206×2622 — the `width`/`height` the pages declare — and quantised with `pngquant --quality=65-92 --speed 1 --strip`, which keeps each file in the 300–870 KB range without visible banding in the theme's gradients. The app's `library` capture is filed here as `notes.png` because the tab is now **Notes**, and its former Habits tab is now **Routines**, so `habits.png` shows Routines.

When the app's screens change, re-export from a fresh capture bundle rather than editing these files, and re-check the pages that name what is on screen: the hero, Read-through and Bible-section `alt` text, the `Free` / `With Him Plus` feature lists on the landing page, and the equivalent sections in `support.html` and `terms.html`. The authoritative split lives in the app's `PlusValueCopy` and `PaywallCopy`: Bible reading, search, highlights, notes, the daily verse, the verse alarm, and the read-through are free; With Him Plus buys the Focus Shield, sermon recording, and account sync.

The current landing page mentions Today's Verse widgets without a separate widget gallery. `assets/widgets/*.png` are real iPhone 17 Pro simulator captures of those widgets on a 1.0.20 build, cropped from the Home Screen, Lock Screen, and Control Center and downscaled to twice their display size: `today-small|medium|large` (transparent rounded corners), `lock-inline`, `lock-rect`, and `control`. The published product also offers optional sermon processing; consult the current privacy policy before making account, cloud, or audio-processing claims on these pages.

## Unfold media and motion

The assets added for Unfold are copied unchanged from the approved prototype, not generated during the production migration:

- `assets/art/HomeVerseFriday.jpg`, `HomeVerseSunday.jpg`, and `OnboardingJesus.png` supply the hero, Reels backdrop, and grace illustration.
- `assets/photos/ending.jpg` is the prototype's illustrative closing image, not a product screenshot or customer testimonial. The scroll-reveal statement uses a plain dark background without a person photo.
- `assets/books/BookArt01.jpg` through `BookArt66.jpg` are the 66 book paintings, in the prototype's gallery order. Every figure and caption is present in `index.html`, not generated by JavaScript.
- `assets/live/{alarm,readthrough,reels,bible}.mp4` and their matching `.jpg` posters are the four actual app recordings from `admin/landing-prototype/assets/live/`. No unused `home` recording is copied. Focus Shield remains the real `assets/screenshots/focus-gate.png` still: there is no Shield recording or `shield.mp4` reference.
- The hero keeps the approved illustrative HTML alarm demo; it is not a recording, microphone input, or a functioning alarm.

`assets/unfold.js` progressively enhances the page. With JavaScript unavailable, the entire copy, 66-book rail, FAQs, and App Store links remain usable, and the day scenes show their individual stills in normal flow. Local **GSAP 3.13.0** and **ScrollTrigger 3.13.0** add the approved native-sticky unfold, word reveal, and scroll progress; if those scripts fail, those sections remain static. The day-stage enhancement and media controller use `IntersectionObserver`, falling back to static scenes/posters when it is unavailable.

Video URLs live only in `data-src` with `preload="none"`. A URL is attached only when that video is visible, its day scene is active (where applicable), the document is visible, demos are enabled, and reduced motion is off. Other videos pause; unavailable footage or rejected autoplay falls back to its poster. Reduced motion is honored on initial load and on preference changes: clips pause and detach, the hero settles, GSAP reverts its transforms/word reveals, and each day scene returns to a static layout. **Pause demos / Play demos** is a keyboard-accessible control for the automatic hero/video demos; scroll-driven effects remain tied to the reader's scrolling. The painting rail supports native touch scrolling, arrow buttons, Left/Right keys, and Home/End; scrolling is immediate under reduced motion. The mobile download bar is hidden from keyboard navigation until visible.

The pinned vendor files are copied from `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/gsap@3.13.0/dist/{gsap,ScrollTrigger}.min.js`, served locally with their copyright headers intact. The applicable Standard "No Charge" GSAP License is included at `assets/vendor/gsap-3.13.0/LICENSE.txt` (source: <https://gsap.com/community/standard-license/>). There is no runtime JavaScript CDN dependency. Fonts still use Google Fonts with system fallbacks.

The landing page retains its production canonical/SEO/social metadata and inert `window.dataLayer` contract: `index_view`, `index_scroll_depth`, `index_app_store_click`, and `index_exit_section`, with `page: 'index'`. Existing CTA locations remain `nav`, `hero`, `pricing-free`, `pricing-habits`, and `close`; the mobile bar adds `sticky`. No analytics provider, tag-manager script, cookie, or network event sink is introduced.

## Build

There is no build step, package installation, or framework. Serve this directory as a static site, for example:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8766 --bind 127.0.0.1
```

Open <http://127.0.0.1:8766/>. The landing page owns its inline styles and `assets/unfold.js`; `assets/shared.css` and `assets/legal.css` continue to serve the supporting pages unchanged.

## Updating the pages

Keep the approved Unfold copy, Cormorant Garamond / Instrument Sans typography, pearl/blush/lavender/night palette, illustrative hero, and scroll choreography aligned with the approved prototype. Adapt production contracts rather than replacing them: preserve the canonical/social URLs, App Store destination, CTA analytics attributes, and legacy anchors. Keep current plan terms aligned across the landing, download, support, and legal pages. Bump the `assets/unfold.js?v=` value after changing its behavior; change the supporting pages' `shared.css?v=` and `legal.css?v=` values only when those styles actually change.

Before release, review desktop (1440px), tablet/split-screen (1024px and 768px), mobile (390px), and narrow (320px) views; keyboard focus, gallery controls, FAQ disclosure, and the sticky CTA; the static no-JS and blocked-vendor states; initial and live-change reduced motion; video lazy loading, active-scene selection, offscreen/document-hidden pausing, and poster fallback; every legacy anchor/App Store link; and the preserved `dataLayer` payloads. A short landscape viewport intentionally uses unpinned unfold/text sections so content does not depend on fitting a full-height phone and headline.
