Grace, not guilt.

A few good questions.

Is the Bible really free?

Yes. You can read the whole Bible without paying for Habits. The optional subscription is for the verse alarm and scroll pause.

Can I use it without internet?

The Bible is available offline, and verse speech recognition runs on your device. Some account or purchase actions may still require a connection.

Do I have to speak my verse aloud?

No. The verse screens also offer a typing option for quiet mornings or places where speaking isn't practical.

Which devices can use With Him?

With Him is available for iPhone on the App Store. Check the listing for the latest iOS compatibility details.

Where do I see subscription terms?

Open the app to review the current price, eligibility, renewal terms, and purchase options before subscribing.
