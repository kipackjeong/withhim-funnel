The honest part

Your phone gets the first word of the day. And the last.

The alarm lives on your phone, so the feed is one swipe away before your eyes are fully open. Your Bible app is on that same phone too, patiently waiting four taps deep.

It isn’t a motivation problem. You already know His Word matters. The feed just gets there first, so With Him gets there first instead.

How it works

Two moments.
Thirty seconds each.

Most Bible apps wait on your home screen to be opened. With Him meets you at the two moments your phone usually wins, and nowhere else.

IAt dawn

Wake with a verse.

An alarm that hands you Scripture, not a snooze button. Read the verse out loud, watch each word light up, and the alarm goes quiet. No passage runs past sixty words.

About 30 secondsOr type it instead
IIMid-scroll

Pause the scroll.

A verse between you and the next hour. Pick the apps to pause. After about five minutes of steady scrolling, a verse appears. Read it aloud and you’re right back in.

You pick the appsOff in three taps, anytime
Why out loud?

You can swipe a notification away half asleep. You can’t say a sentence out loud without being present for it.

Grace, not guilt

A doorbell, not a toll gate.

Reading a verse at 6:40 earns you nothing, from God or from the app. It simply puts His Word in front of you at the moment you’d have missed it, then gets out of the way.

  • No streaks to break

    Miss a day or a month. Nothing scolds you, and nothing chases you.

  • No failure screens

    No red X, no score, no guilt-trip notification about a day you missed.

  • Honest about its limits

    iOS lets you force-quit any app. With Him doesn’t make skipping impossible, just a little harder than thirty seconds of reading.

Threads

Not a random verse. A journey.

Your daily verse comes from a Thread: a hand-written sequence of passages on one theme, read in order. Wake with a verse and Pause the scroll both draw from it, so every reading moves you forward.

A few good questions.

Is the Bible really free?

Yes. You can read the whole Bible without paying for Habits. The optional subscription is for the verse alarm and scroll pause.

Can I use it without internet?

The Bible is available offline, and verse speech recognition runs on your device. Some account or purchase actions may still require a connection.

Do I have to speak my verse aloud?

No. The verse screens also offer a typing option for quiet mornings or places where speaking isn't practical.

Which devices can use With Him?

With Him is available for iPhone on the App Store. Check the listing for the latest iOS compatibility details.

Where do I see subscription terms?

Open the app to review the current price, eligibility, renewal terms, and purchase options before subscribing.
