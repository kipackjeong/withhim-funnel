A doorbell,
not a toll gate.

Reading a verse at 6:30 earns you nothing, from God or from the app. It puts His Word in front of you when you’d have missed it, then gets out of the way.

  • No streaks to break

    Miss a day or a month. You won’t get a red X, a score, or a guilt-trip notification.

  • Honest about its limits

    iOS lets you force-quit any app, this one included. With Him only makes skipping a little harder than thirty seconds of reading.

  • Your voice stays on your iPhone

    Your iPhone checks the reading on the device. The audio isn’t saved or sent anywhere.

A few good questions.

Do I have to read out loud?

No. You can type the verse instead.

Is my verse reading recorded?

No. Your iPhone checks the reading on the device, and the audio isn’t saved or sent anywhere.

Does it work without internet?

Reading the Bible and finishing a verse both work offline. Signing in and purchases need a connection.

Which devices can use With Him?
