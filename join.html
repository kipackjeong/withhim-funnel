What founding members actually get

Four things, all of them deliverable by one person, and all of them written here so they can be held against him later.

1.A permanent price lock

Whatever you pay when you subscribe, that's what you pay for as long as you keep it. Prices go up as products grow; yours won't. This applies to any tier you choose.

2.The founding lifetime rate — $49 instead of $79.99

One payment, forever, at $49 against a planned lifetime price of $79.99.

Two real limits on this, stated plainly rather than buried. It's available only in the first week after launch, and only to the first 500 people on this list. That number isn't a marketing device — it's set at a level one developer can support for a lifetime cohort without it becoming a burden, and there's no counter on this page telling you it's running out, because that would be theatre.

3.Early TestFlight access, in waves

Builds go out to waitlist members before launch, in waves, with the first wave deliberately small. Apple caps external testing and reviews every beta build, so "waves" is the honest word rather than "immediate."

You'll be asked before you're added to one. Nobody gets an unrequested beta.

4.A vote on which Threads get written first

Twelve Threads have to be authored before launch — each one an ordered sequence of passages on a single theme, written and reviewed by hand. The order they're written in is genuinely undecided, so waitlist members rank them and the queue follows the ranking.

It's a real vote on real work, and it's also the cheapest market research available. Both things are true and it seems better to say so.

What you are not signing up for

  • No payment now. There is no card field on this page and no charge of any kind before launch.
  • No launch date. Because there isn't one that would be honest. See below.
  • No obligation. If the launch email lands and you've moved on, ignore it. Nothing is owed.
  • No sharing your email. Not sold, not rented, not passed to anyone.

Why no money is being taken now

Pre-selling an unbuilt app is legal and common. It's not happening here for one specific reason: the Focus Shield needs Apple to approve an entitlement, on a timeline nobody outside Apple controls, and there is a real chance it's delayed or refused. Taking money for a feature that might not ship isn't a risk worth running.

So: emails now, money only when there's something to buy.

What it will cost at launch

Published here months ahead of time, so that moving it later would be visible.

  • Free, permanently — the entire Bible offline, whole-Bible search, highlights, notes, bookmarks, cross-references, and the daily Thread verse. Never time-limited, never a trial.
  • $4.99 / month — the Voice-Dismissed Alarm and the Focus Shield.
  • $29.99 / year — the same, with a 7-day free trial.
  • $79.99 once — lifetime. $49 for founding members, first week after launch, first 500 on this list.

No verse, book, or search result is ever behind a payment. The subscription buys the alarm and the shield and nothing else.

Before you submit — this is iPhone only

Version one runs on iPhone, on iOS 26 or later. There is no Android version, no iPad-optimised layout, no web app, and no Watch app, and none of those are near-term plans.

If you're on Android, joining this list would mean waiting a long time for something that may never come to your phone. It's better to say that now than to have you find out in a launch email. If you'd like to be told if Android ever happens, join and say "Android" in the free-text field, and you'll be kept separate from the launch list.

What happens to what you just typed

Your email is used to send you build invitations, the launch note, and occasional progress updates. It is not sold, not rented, not shared with anyone, and not used to build a profile of you anywhere.

Your Thread ranking is counted. Your free-text answer is read by one person and goes no further.

Separately, and worth repeating here: the app itself has no account and no server. Nothing you read, highlight, or note inside it ever reaches anyone. This waitlist is an email list and it is the only place your address exists.

Unsubscribe from any email in one click. That removes you from everything, including the founding offer, with no follow-up.
